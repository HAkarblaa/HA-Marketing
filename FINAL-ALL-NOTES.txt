HA Marketing — consolidated final package

Included in this site package:
1) Accounts/auth/profile flow already built with Supabase.
2) Real service orders, employee workflow, location sharing/tracking and ratings.
3) In-app notifications + Firebase Web Push wiring and service worker.
4) Real shop catalog/cart/orders/admin flow. Payment remains cash-on-delivery until a real payment merchant/provider is connected.

8) Admin pages manage employee approval, service orders, and shop administration.
9) Password recovery cannot be securely automated by phone while accounts use synthetic @ha.local email unless a verified SMS/WhatsApp provider is connected. A real provider is intentionally not faked.
10) Religious/entertainment pages are preserved in this consolidated package.
